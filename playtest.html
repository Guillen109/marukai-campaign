What to test

  1. Download and launch: does the game install or start correctly? Are the available fighters and arenas visible?
  2. Controls: try the keyboard and, if possible, a controller. Check movement, jumps, dashes and blocking.
  3. Combat: try combos, throws and special moves. Is the action readable and enjoyable?
  4. Physics and camera: look for floating feet, ground collision bugs, hitbox issues and offscreen fighters.
  5. Performance: note stutters, crashes, audio problems and loading delays.
  6. Modes: test Arcade and local Versus if they are available in your build.

What to include in a report

Tell us the download/build date, Windows version, CPU, GPU, RAM, controller, game mode and character. For each bug: what you did, what you expected, what happened and how to reproduce it. Optional screenshots or short clips help.

Please don't send passwords, account numbers, private files or system logs containing personal details. You can stop testing at any time.

Feedback questions

  1. How long did it take you to start playing?
  2. Which fighter did you choose and why?
  3. Did any bug prevent you from playing?
  4. Do hits, movement and reactions feel clear?
  5. What was the best and worst part of the experience?
  6. Would you play again or recommend it? Why?

Send your answers via email or through an authorized playtesting platform.
